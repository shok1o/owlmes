1General

These terms are the rules for using Owl Messenger (the website owlmes.ru and the app installed from it; “Owl”). Owl is run by shok1o, an independent developer, as a free, non-commercial project. By signing up for or using Owl, you accept these terms and the Privacy Policy.

2Your account

  • You are responsible for keeping your password safe and for what is done from your account.
  • Nicknames are unique. Don't take a nickname to pretend to be another person, a brand or the Owl administration.

4Moderation

  • You can report a message or a user with the flag button. Reports are reviewed by the administrator, and the reporter stays anonymous.
  • For violations, the administrator may delete a message, or block or delete an account.
  • A blocked user sees the reason and can send an appeal right from the app.
  • You can also block anyone yourself — then they can no longer message you.

5Your content

  • Your messages, photos and voice messages remain yours. You allow Owl to store them and show them to recipients — the messenger can't work otherwise.
  • You are responsible for what you send and must not infringe other people's rights, including copyright.
  • A deleted message disappears for everyone in the conversation.
