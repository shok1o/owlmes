1Who we are

This policy explains how Owl Messenger (the website owlmes.ru and the app installed from it; “Owl”, “we”) processes personal data. It is written to meet the requirements of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Controller: shok1o, an independent developer based in Russia, who runs Owl as a free, non-commercial project.
Contact for any privacy request: privacy@owlmes.ru

3Purposes and legal bases

  • Providing the messenger — sign-up, sign-in, delivering messages, calls and notifications. Legal basis: performance of our agreement with you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Owl on Guard — alerting the people you chose if you don't check in by the deadline. It only runs when you start a timer yourself. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR); you can stop the timer at any time.
  • Security and moderation — protection against spam, bots, fraud and abuse, handling reports. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in a safe service (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Call reliability — if a call fails, the app sends a short technical report: which step failed and with what error, the app version and the device type. It contains no names, texts or addresses — it's impossible to tell from it who called whom. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in calls that work (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Push notifications — only after you allow them on your device. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time in settings.
  • Service emails — confirmation codes and password resets. Legal basis: performance of the agreement.
  • Legal obligations — where the law requires us to act (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).

Your data is not used for advertising, profiling or automated decisions that affect your rights (except for automatic limits against abuse — for example, the protection against excessive call traffic, see section 5).

4Who can see your data

  • Other users — your profile (according to your privacy settings) and your messages in shared chats and conversations with you.
  • The administrator — your email and technical account information. The administrator has technical access to the database, but conversations are not read without a reason: a message is looked at only if it was reported or a rule violation is suspected.
  • The person who reports someone stays anonymous to the reported user.
  • The person you're on a call with — usually your voice goes through a relay server, and the other person can't see your IP address. If a connection through the relay fails, Owl tries to connect the devices directly — your device's IP address is then technically available to the other person. In a group call, voice and video always go only through the relay server: participants can't see each other's IP addresses, and a group call is never connected directly. The video of a participant you don't follow is hidden until you tap “Show video”.
  • Group members — while a group call is in progress, they can see who started it and who is on the call right now. When the call starts, group members get a notification, and a call card from the person who started it appears in the group — it is kept like a regular message.
  • Your people in Owl on Guard — they receive nothing while the timer is running. If you don't tap “I'm OK” by the deadline, they get your nickname, the timer text and the deadline in your private chat with them — and a message that all is well once you check in.

We don't sell your data or share it for advertising. We may disclose it to public authorities only where the law clearly requires it.

5Service providers and international transfers

Owl uses the following providers, which process data on our behalf (processors):

Google FirebaseSign-in, storage of profiles and messages, bot protection (reCAPTCHA). The database is located in the EU (Belgium and the Netherlands).
CloudflareDomain, bot protection (Turnstile), the push-notification server and its storage (device addresses, quiet hours, language, Owl on Guard timers), and relay servers for calls (they see IP addresses but can't listen to the conversation). To stop the call relay servers from being misused, we pass Cloudflare a pseudonymous account label — without your name or email — together with the password for the relay. Cloudflare uses the label to measure the amount of traffic, and we can see if someone uses too much of it. If your account uses more than 5 GB within an hour, your passwords for the relay server are revoked automatically and the relay server is unavailable to you for 24 hours: group calls don't work, and one-to-one calls are only possible with people you follow and without a relay server on your side — your device's IP address is then technically available to the other person. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in protecting the call relay servers from abuse and excessive costs (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
ResendSending confirmation codes and password-reset emails (EU region).
GitHub PagesHosting of the website files. Like any web server, it sees your IP address when you open the site.
Apple, Google, MozillaPush delivery. The content is encrypted — only your device can read it.

Some of these providers are based in the USA. Transfers rely on the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework and/or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. Owl is operated from Russia, a country without an EU adequacy decision: your data is stored in the EU, and the administrator may access it remotely for support and moderation.

6How long we keep data

  • Profile and messages — until you delete them or your account.
  • A deleted message is removed from the database for all chat members at once.
  • When you delete your account, your profile, nickname, email, private conversations and notifications are erased. Messages in group chats may remain without a link to your profile.
  • Your device's push address is deleted when you turn notifications off or sign out.
  • The technical call record (who is calling whom and the connection data) is deleted right after the call; if a call ends abnormally, it is deleted automatically within 24 hours. The call note in the conversation is kept like a regular message.
  • The technical record of a group call (who is on the call and the connection data) is deleted when the call ends. Anything the app didn't manage to delete on its own (for example, because the app was closed in the middle of a call or the call was cut off) is deleted automatically no more than 2 hours later.
  • The internal record of passwords for the relay server (the pseudonymous label, the password's internal name and its expiry time) is deleted when the password expires or is revoked: up to 3 hours for a one-to-one call and up to 12 hours for a group call, plus up to an hour for clean-up.
  • An Owl on Guard timer and its text are deleted as soon as you tap “I'm OK”. If an alert was sent — after 24 hours; in any case no later than two days after the deadline. Alerts sent to your people are kept like regular messages in your conversation.
  • Call failure reports are kept in the server's technical log for no longer than 7 days. The pseudonymous account label is also recorded there if the protection against excessive traffic is triggered.
  • Email confirmation codes expire after 15 minutes; the “while you were sleeping” summary (names only, no message text) is deleted once it has been sent in the morning.

7How we protect data

  • the connection to Owl is always encrypted (HTTPS);
  • access is restricted by database rules: everyone can only read their own chats, and your email is stored separately and closed to others;
  • push notifications are encrypted with your device's key;
  • voice, video and screen sharing in calls are encrypted on the devices (DTLS-SRTP): the relay server passes them on but can't decrypt them. Calls are never recorded;
  • sign-up is protected against bots, and nobody can send a notification in someone else's name.

8Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your data, have it corrected or erased, restrict or object to processing, receive your data in a portable format (portability) and withdraw consent at any time. Many of these you can do yourself:

Edit your dataNickname, photo and bio — in settings.
Control who sees whatSettings → Privacy, or the “Owl's Eye” button.
Turn off notificationsIn settings, at any time.
Limit who can call youSettings → Privacy → “Who can call me”: all, people I follow, or nobody.
Stop Owl on GuardThe “I'm OK” button — the timer and its text are deleted right away.
Delete your accountSettings → “Delete account forever”.

For anything else — for example a copy of your data — write to privacy@owlmes.ru. We answer within one month.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the EU country where you live (in Germany — the authority of your federal state; in Poland — UODO, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office).

9Age

In the EU, Owl may be used from the age of 16. If you are younger, you may only use Owl with the consent of a parent or legal guardian.

10Data on your device

Owl stores your settings and a copy of loaded chats on your device so that the app opens faster and works offline. This is strictly necessary for the app to work. There are no advertising or tracking cookies.
